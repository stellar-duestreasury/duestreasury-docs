# Privacy

On-chain data includes public addresses, token contract ids, numeric group/proposal ids, token amounts, period markers, signer approvals, and opaque memo hashes. A hash does not guarantee anonymity: predictable text can be guessed. Keep group descriptions and spending evidence off-chain, and use synthetic demonstration data only.

Never put organization names, contacts, emails, identity numbers or free text in chain payloads. The app has no backend, trackers or third-party scripts. Wallet icons are local; selected wallet providers and configured RPC/explorer services may still make network requests. The app never asks for a secret key or seed phrase.
